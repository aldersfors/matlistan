# Notifications when the week is ready

Matlistan can tell your phone when the Sunday draft is ready, so you know to open the app
and approve the week. Each device is turned on separately, and anyone in the family can
turn it on for their own phone.

## Turn it on

1. On an iPhone or iPad, open Matlistan from the Home Screen. Notifications do not work in
   a Safari tab. If Matlistan is not on your Home Screen yet: in Safari, tap Share, then
   Add to Home Screen. This needs iOS 16.4 or later.
2. Open **Settings**, find **Aviseringar** and tap **Slå på på den här enheten**.
3. Allow notifications when the phone asks.

On a computer, the same button works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

## What you get

One notification when the Sunday job has made a new draft: "Veckans förslag är klart".
Tapping it opens that week. Nothing else sends notifications.

## Turn it off

Open **Settings**, **Aviseringar**, and tap **Stäng av på den här enheten**. You can also
turn notifications off for Matlistan in the phone's settings; Matlistan then stops trying
after the next Sunday.

## If nothing arrives

- The section says to open Matlistan from the Home Screen: you are in a Safari tab.
- The section says notifications are blocked: allow them for Matlistan in the phone's or
  browser's settings, then tap the button again.
- The button worked but nothing came on Sunday: the week may already have had a draft. The
  job only notifies when it makes a new one.
